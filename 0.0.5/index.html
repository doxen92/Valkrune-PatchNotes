Balance · Impact

No More One-Combo Kills

Burst combo (Dash + Heavy + Basic + Hero) as a percentage of target's max HP
Before · v0.0.4 burst
After · v0.0.5 burst
What this means: Before v0.0.5, a stacked burst combo at L1 dealt 108% of max HP — instant kill if anything connected. Even at L10, a single burst chewed through 60% of an enemy's pool. After v0.0.5, the entire curve sits below the 50% line from L5 onward. One-combo kills are mathematically eliminated at mid-late game. Players who land the full burst still hurt hard — they just can't delete you in a single window.

New System · Survival

Spawn Protection

2-second post-respawn invulnerability — gold dome VFX makes it obvious to both attackers and defenders

Two seconds. Then it's on.

Procedural hemisphere with a pulsing gold ring at the base. Server-authoritative end-time syncs to all clients via NetworkVariable so every peer sees the protection window identically.

  • 2 seconds of full invulnerability after respawn
  • Gold dome VFX — visible to everyone in the match
  • Self-breaks if you attack — no shield-and-stab exploits
  • Attacker also loses their own protection on retaliation
  • Server-authoritative via NetworkVariable<double> end-time
  • Procedural shader — no expensive asset to ship

Known Issues

Open in v0.0.5

One reproducibility issue + a few minor cosmetic items still being investigated
  • Investigating Client may spawn at map center on match start — happens roughly 25% of matches and is not consistently reproducible. Workaround: die once; respawn places you at the correct slot.
  • Tracking Disconnected client may leave a ghost prefab visible to other peers until the next match. Cosmetic — gameplay unaffected.
  • Cosmetic Spawn-protection VFX duration can feel inconsistent in rare edge cases. The actual invulnerability window is correct; only the visual end-time is off.
