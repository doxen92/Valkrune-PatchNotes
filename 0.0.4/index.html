Centralization

GameDebugConfig Owns Diagnostics

Every Inspector verboseLogs field was physically deleted from its component and replaced with a single GameDebugConfig flag. Six new audio flags plus six combat / movement flags now live behind a single masterEnable kill-switch.

  • StrikeAudioDebug strike broadcasts
  • HitAudioDebug hit / shield / bleed
  • DashAudioDebug 2D + 3D dash
  • MusicDebug state transitions
  • StingAudioDebug match-end stings
  • AudioPoolDebug voice stealing
  • NameHudDebug player name resolve
  • KnockbackDebug apply + ended
Known Issues

Tracked for v0.0.5

Open items being worked before — or just after — alpha launch
  • Critical Mobile performance not yet validated — APK build + on-device FPS measurement is the alpha-blocker. Frame budget target: under 33ms (30fps minimum) on a mid-tier 2024 device.
  • High Vortex Pull slow/debuff lost — a side-effect of the double-spawn fix. The pull itself works; the post-pull slow needs to be re-applied from the new single broadcast path.
  • High Multiplayer stress test pending — 3 humans + 6 bots on a real Relay connection, sustained for a full match. Needed before opening alpha to wider testing.
  • Low TalentManager / LevelScaledStats race — both write cachedHealth.maxHealth in Update. TalentManager wins in practice; not blocking, but on the post-alpha cleanup list.
  • Low Whirlwind hit-audio not yet implemented — final audio feature on the backlog. Post-alpha.
